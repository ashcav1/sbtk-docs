    Internal functions

    Last edited October 20, 2026

    Functions in sbtk.internal implement the supported functions. They are not part of the supported interface: their arguments and behavior may change without notice, and scripts should not call them. They are listed here because the methods pages refer to some of them. The toolbox contains no deprecated or legacy functions.

    FunctionPurposeUsed by (supported functions)
    sbtk.internal.body_samplesPoints that fill the body, with the volume each one represents.sbtk.compare.reference, sbtk.data.simulate, sbtk.gravity.random_field_integrals
    sbtk.internal.body_settingsThe numbers that define a body, for settings.json.sbtk.io.report_comparison, sbtk.io.report_inversion, sbtk.io.report_simulation
    sbtk.internal.check_rotationStop unless axes is a proper rotation (orthonormal columns, right-handed).sbtk.body.to_frame, sbtk.data.to_frame, sbtk.interior.to_frame
    sbtk.internal.component_columnsDesign-matrix columns and volumes of components idx at their current geometry.sbtk.invert.run, sbtk.invert.setup
    sbtk.internal.component_memberTrue for points X inside component c.sbtk.compare.reference, sbtk.exchange.density_grid, sbtk.gravity.component_integrals
    sbtk.internal.component_settingsComponents as plain records for settings.json (only the fields each type uses).sbtk.io.report_inversion, sbtk.io.report_simulation
    sbtk.internal.component_textOne-line description of a component's geometry (printouts and text files).sbtk.exchange.write_model, sbtk.exchange.write_solution, sbtk.io.write_truth
    sbtk.internal.ComputePolyhedralCSBar_mexFully normalized spherical-harmonic coefficients of a uniform tetrahedron with one vertex at the expansion origin (compiled C).sbtk.gravity.geometry
    sbtk.internal.density_at_pointsDensity [kg/m^3] of a model at points inside the body.sbtk.compare.reference, sbtk.data.simulate, sbtk.exchange.density_grid, sbtk.interior.density_at
    sbtk.internal.density_mapSequential dark-blue -> teal -> yellow color map (viridis-like) for densities.sbtk.plot.slices, sbtk.plot.truth_vs_estimate
    sbtk.internal.difference_mapBlue - white - red color map for signed differences (blue lighter, red denser).sbtk.plot.interior3d, sbtk.plot.truth_vs_estimate
    sbtk.internal.empty_moiEmpty list of moment-of-inertia measurement rows.sbtk.data.from_coefficients, sbtk.exchange.read_data, sbtk.io.read_jpl_gravity, sbtk.io.read_measurements
    sbtk.internal.exchange_conventionsText of conventions.txt: units, frame, normalization (with code), degree 1.other internal functions
    sbtk.internal.exchange_data_filesWrite the measurement files of an exchange package.sbtk.exchange.write_data, sbtk.exchange.write_model
    sbtk.internal.exchange_grid_writeWrite density_grid.txt of an interior-model or solution package.sbtk.exchange.write_model, sbtk.exchange.write_solution
    sbtk.internal.exchange_numberNumeric value of a metadata key of an exchange file (error if missing).sbtk.exchange.read_data, sbtk.exchange.read_grid
    sbtk.internal.exchange_readRead one exchange-format text file (see sbtk.internal.exchange_write).sbtk.exchange.read_data, sbtk.exchange.read_grid
    sbtk.internal.exchange_writeWrite one exchange-format text file.sbtk.exchange.write_model, sbtk.exchange.write_solution
    sbtk.internal.fixed_pointIterate x = update(x) until a step moves x by at most tol; at most max_iter steps.sbtk.data.simulate, sbtk.interior.align_with_spin_axis
    sbtk.internal.geometry_deviationHow far one geometry parameter of a component is from its start.sbtk.invert.run
    sbtk.internal.geometry_parametersThe geometry an inversion estimates: one entry per scalar parameter.sbtk.invert.setup
    sbtk.internal.geometry_stepA component with one geometry parameter changed by a step.sbtk.invert.run
    sbtk.internal.icosphereUnit sphere mesh: an icosahedron with every triangle split into four LEVEL times.sbtk.shape.ellipsoid
    sbtk.internal.inertia_from_QInertia tensor from second moments.sbtk.exchange.read_data, sbtk.gravity.forward
    sbtk.internal.libration_weightsWeights on [Ixx Iyy Izz Ixy Ixz Iyz] of the libration row (B - A) - ratio*C = 0.sbtk.data.add_libration, sbtk.data.simulate, sbtk.data.to_frame
    sbtk.internal.moi_tensor3x3 normalized inertia tensor from measurement rows Ixx..Iyz (NaN if any is missing).sbtk.data.add_moi_row, sbtk.data.add_noise, sbtk.data.select_moi, sbtk.data.simulate, sbtk.data.to_frame, sbtk.exchange.read_data, sbtk.io.read_measurements
    sbtk.internal.new_componentFields shared by every component (use the sbtk.interior constructors).sbtk.interior.ellipsoid, sbtk.interior.layer, sbtk.interior.no_components, sbtk.interior.sector, sbtk.interior.sphere, sbtk.interior.torus
    sbtk.internal.observePredicted measurements from per-unit-density integrals I (struct with U and Q).sbtk.invert.run, sbtk.invert.setup
    sbtk.internal.point_mass_shNormalized spherical harmonics of unit point masses.sbtk.gravity.component_integrals
    sbtk.internal.principal_axesPrincipal axes of inertia from the degree-2 gravity coefficients.sbtk.exchange.write_model, sbtk.gravity.center_of_mass_frame, sbtk.plot.difference_from_homogeneous
    sbtk.internal.random_field_valueRandom-field density perturbation [kg/m^3] at points X (n x 3, km).sbtk.gravity.random_field_integrals
    sbtk.internal.read_objTriangular faces and vertices of a Wavefront OBJ shape model.sbtk.shape.load
    sbtk.internal.rotate_inertiaInertia elements [Ixx Iyy Izz Ixy Ixz Iyz] in rotated axes: axes' * I * axes.sbtk.data.to_frame, sbtk.interior.to_frame
    sbtk.internal.sh_packingOrdering of packed spherical-harmonic coefficient vectors.sbtk.data.from_coefficients, sbtk.data.select_degree, sbtk.data.set_uncertainties, sbtk.data.summary, sbtk.data.to_frame, sbtk.data.use_uncertainties, sbtk.exchange.read_shadr, sbtk.exchange.write_model, sbtk.gravity.center_of_mass_frame, sbtk.gravity.geometry, sbtk.gravity.labels, sbtk.interior.to_frame, sbtk.io.read_jpl_gravity, sbtk.io.report_simulation, sbtk.io.write_measurements, sbtk.plot.difference_from_homogeneous, sbtk.plot.measurements
    sbtk.internal.sh_rotationMatrix that expresses spherical-harmonic coefficients in rotated axes.sbtk.data.to_frame, sbtk.gravity.center_of_mass_frame, sbtk.interior.to_frame, sbtk.plot.difference_from_homogeneous
    sbtk.internal.sh_translationMatrix that moves spherical-harmonic integrals to a shifted expansion origin.sbtk.exchange.write_model, sbtk.gravity.center_of_mass_frame, sbtk.invert.run
    sbtk.internal.shape_file_nameFile name of a shape model in an exchange package.other internal functions
    sbtk.internal.slice_axesDraw one density slice (image + body outline) on axes ax.sbtk.plot.slices, sbtk.plot.truth_vs_estimate
    sbtk.internal.slice_imagesAbsolute density of each model on the two slice planes.sbtk.plot.slices, sbtk.plot.truth_vs_estimate
    sbtk.internal.slice_planesGrid points on the two plotting planes, for density slices.other internal functions
    sbtk.internal.surface_distanceDistance from the expansion origin to the surface along unit directions [km].sbtk.exchange.density_grid, sbtk.gravity.component_integrals, sbtk.interior.density_at, sbtk.invert.run, sbtk.plot.interior3d
    sbtk.internal.surface_distance_nearDistance from a nearby origin to the surface through given points [km].sbtk.compare.reference
