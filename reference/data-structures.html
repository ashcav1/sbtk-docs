    Data structures

    Last edited October 21, 2026

    SBTK passes MATLAB structures between its functions. Each structure has a fixed set of fields, listed below; the field names carry their units (see Units). Sizes refer to the Didymos examples (49,152 facets, degree 4) unless stated: n is the number of measurements, nc the number of components, nf the number of facets, L the highest degree.

    A model is any structure with the fields rho_bg_kgm3, components, random_field and origin_km: a true model (truth) or an inversion result (fit). Functions that evaluate, plot or compare densities accept either.

    shape: Shape model#

    Created by sbtk.shape.load, sbtk.shape.bundled, sbtk.shape.from_arrays, sbtk.shape.ellipsoid, sbtk.shape.reduce, sbtk.shape.reduce_spc.

    A closed triangular mesh with vertex positions in kilometers.

    Fields of shape (6)
    FieldSizeUnitDescription
    name#textFile name without extension, or the name given.
    faces#nf × 3Vertex indices of each triangle (1-based), ordered so that the normal points outward.
    vertices_km#nv × 3kmVertex positions in the shape-model frame.
    source#textPath of the file the shape was read from ('arrays' or 'ellipsoid' for shapes not read from a file). After sbtk.body.to_frame, the name of the original file without its path. sbtk.exchange.write_data copies the file into a measurement package if the shape model is that file unchanged.
    frame_origin_km#1 × 3kmOrigin of the shape model's frame in the coordinates of source: [0 0 0], or as set by sbtk.body.to_frame (combined over successive changes of frame).
    frame_axes#3 × 3Axes of the shape model's frame (columns) in the coordinates of source: eye(3), or as set by sbtk.body.to_frame. The vertices are (source vertices - frame_origin_km)*frame_axes.

    component: Interior component#

    Created by sbtk.interior.sphere, sbtk.interior.ellipsoid, sbtk.interior.torus, sbtk.interior.layer, sbtk.interior.sector, sbtk.interior.sector_grid.

    One region of uniform relative density. Every component has every field, so components of different types can be concatenated into one structure array ([c1, c2]); fields that a type does not use are zero. The geometry definitions are in Density distributions.

    Fields of component (17)
    FieldSizeUnitDescription
    name#textName; unique within a model.
    type#text'sphere', 'ellipsoid', 'torus', 'layer' or 'sector'.
    drho_kgm3#scalarkg/m³Relative density: density minus the background density. In an inversion it is estimated unless estimate_density is false.
    center_km#1 × 3kmCenter of a sphere or ellipsoid in the shape-model frame; for a torus, the point on its axis from which height_km is measured ([0 0 0] unless changed by sbtk.interior.to_frame).
    radius_km#scalarkmSphere radius.
    semi_axes_km#1 × 3kmEllipsoid semi-axes along its own axes.
    axes#3 × 3Columns: the component's own axes in the shape-model frame. Ellipsoid: Rz(yaw)Ry(pitch)Rx(roll). Torus (about its third axis) and sector (latitude and longitude in these axes): eye(3) unless changed by sbtk.interior.to_frame.
    ring_radius_km#scalarkmTorus: radius of the circle at the center of the tube.
    tube_radius_km#scalarkmTorus: radius of the tube.
    height_km#scalarkmTorus: distance of the plane of the center circle from center_km along the torus axis (z for a torus made by sbtk.interior.torus).
    depth_km#1 × 2kmLayer: top and bottom depth below the surface, measured along rays from the expansion origin.
    frac#1 × 2Sector: inner and outer fraction of the distance from the expansion origin to the surface (0 at the origin, 1 at the surface).
    lat_deg#1 × 2degSector: latitude range, within −90 to 90.
    lon_deg#1 × 2degSector: longitude range, within −360 to 360 and at most 360 wide; ranges beyond ±180 wrap.
    estimate_density#logicalTrue (set by the constructors): the inversion estimates drho_kgm3. False: the value is held fixed (a prescribed component).
    estimate_position#logical, 1 or 1 × 3Spheres, ellipsoids and tori. False (set by the constructors). True: the inversion also estimates center_km; a mask [x y z] estimates only those coordinates, e.g. [false false true] for the height (Estimated geometry).
    estimate_axis#logicalEllipsoids and tori. False (set by the constructors). True: the inversion also estimates the orientation: for a torus the direction of its axis (the third of axes), for an ellipsoid also its turn about that axis.

    interior: Interior model#

    Created by sbtk.interior.create.

    Input of sbtk.data.simulate and sbtk.gravity.forward. The background density is not part of it: it follows from the mass of the body.

    Fields of interior (2)
    FieldSizeUnitDescription
    components#1 × nc structThe component array.
    random_field#structThe random_field; standard deviation 0 means no field.

    random_field: Gaussian random density field#

    Created by sbtk.interior.random_field, sbtk.interior.no_random_field.

    A sum of 300 Fourier modes with random wave vectors and phases, δρ(𝐱)=a∑j=1300cos(𝐤j·𝐱+φj) with a=σ2/300 and 𝐤j drawn from a normal distribution with standard deviation 1/L per component, which gives the correlation exp(−|Δ𝐱|2/(2L2)). The field is defined at every point and does not depend on the expansion origin.

    Fields of random_field (6)
    FieldSizeUnitDescription
    sigma_kgm3#scalarkg/m³Standard deviation σ of the density perturbation; 0 means no field.
    corr_length_km#scalarkmCorrelation length L.
    seed#scalarSeed of the random number stream; the same seed gives the same field.
    k_per_km#300 × 31/kmWave vectors 𝐤j.
    phase#300 × 1radPhases φj.
    amp_kgm3#scalarkg/m³Amplitude a of each mode.

    unc: Uncertainties for a simulation#

    Created by sbtk.data.uncertainties.

    1σ uncertainties by degree and by inertia quantity, which sbtk.data.set_uncertainties gives to a measurement set (for example the exact values of sbtk.data.simulate, which have none) and sbtk.data.add_noise then draws noise from.

    Fields of unc (3)
    FieldSizeUnitDescription
    coef_sigma_by_degree#1 × L1σ of every fully normalized coefficient of degree 1, 2, …, L. For degree 1, sbtk.units.offset_to_degree1 converts a center-of-mass uncertainty in km.
    moi_sigma#scalar1σ of each inertia element divided by MRref2.
    libration_sigma#scalar1σ of the libration ratio (B−A)/C.

    obs: Measurement set#

    Gravity coefficients with their covariance, moment-of-inertia measurements, mass, reference radius and expansion origin. See Measurement sets.

    FieldSizeUnitDescription

    name#textName of the data set (for packages, the package name).

    GM_km3_s2#scalarkm³/s²GM.
    R_ref_km#scalarkmReference radius of the coefficients.
    degree#scalarHighest degree L.
    origin_km#1 × 3kmExpansion origin in the shape-model frame.

    com_minus_cof_km#1 × 3kmCenter of mass minus center of figure.

    moi: Moment-of-inertia measurement row#

    Created by sbtk.data.add_moi, sbtk.data.add_moi_row, sbtk.data.add_libration, sbtk.data.simulate.

    One measurement of a linear combination of the inertia elements: 𝐰·[Ixx,Iyy,Izz,Ixy,Ixz,Iyz]/(MRref2)=value±σ.

    Fields of moi (4)
    FieldSizeUnitDescription
    label#text'Ixx', 'Iyy', 'Izz', 'Ixy', 'Ixz', 'Iyz', 'libration', or a name given to sbtk.data.add_moi_row.
    weights#1 × 6Weights 𝐰 on [Ixx,Iyy,Izz,Ixy,Ixz,Iyz]. Single elements have one weight 1; the libration row is [−cos(2a),cos(2a),−k,2sin(2a),0,0] (Moments of inertia).
    value#scalarMeasured value (0 for the libration row).
    sigma#scalar1σ (for the libration row, the 1σ of the ratio times Izz/(MRref2)); NaN for exact values without uncertainties.
