    Shape models#

    A shape model is a closed triangular mesh, stored in SBTK as a shape structure with vertices in km.

    Reading and creating shape models#

    Function Input
    sbtk.shape.load Wavefront OBJ file: v x y z and f i j k records; normals, texture coordinates and faces written as f i/t/n are accepted, other records ignored. The length unit of the file ("km", "m" or "cm") is a required argument.
    sbtk.shape.bundled A model shipped with SBTK, by name (table below).
    sbtk.shape.from_arrays Face and vertex arrays with their length unit.
    sbtk.shape.ellipsoid A triaxial ellipsoid: an icosahedron subdivided level times (20·4^level facets) with vertices projected onto the ellipsoid.
    sbtk.shape.write_obj Writes a shape model as OBJ with vertices in km.

    Requirements#

    The forward model integrates over the cones from the expansion origin to every facet (Gravity and moment calculations). The mesh must therefore be

    • closed: every edge is shared by exactly two facets that traverse it in opposite directions;
    • ordered outward: the enclosed volume computed from the facets is positive;
    • star-shaped about the expansion origin: every facet faces away from it, so that each ray from the origin crosses the surface once.

    sbtk.shape.properties reports all three, the last about the centroid (props). sbtk.gravity.geometry stops with sbtk:gravity:notStarShaped when a facet faces the expansion origin.

    MATLAB script: Checking a shape model
    run("/path/to/SBTK/sbtk_setup.m")
shape = sbtk.shape.bundled("didymos");
p = sbtk.shape.properties(shape)
fprintf("volume %.5f km^3, centroid [%.1f %.1f %.1f] m\n", p.volume_km3, 1e3*p.centroid_km)

    Differences of this size between the shape used to simulate data and the shape used to invert them act as a systematic error in the coefficients. Measurement packages therefore include the shape model they were made with (Exchange packages).

    Reducing high-resolution models#

    High-resolution shape models have more facets than coefficients to degree 4 or 5 require. Two functions reduce them:

    • sbtk.shape.reduce_spc keeps every step-th grid point of an SPC model stored in its grid order (6 cube faces of (q+1)2 vertices, 12q2 facets), triangulated as the original. The grid is radial, so the result stays star-shaped about the body's center; a cell whose triangles would face the center is split along its other diagonal.
    • sbtk.shape.reduce applies MATLAB's reducepatch to any mesh and checks that the result is closed, outward and star-shaped about its centroid; a general reduction can fold facets, in which case it stops with sbtk:shape:reduce.
    MATLAB
    big   = sbtk.shape.load("g_00243mm_spc_obj_dimo_0000n00000_v004.obj", "km");   % q = 512, 3,145,728 facets
small = sbtk.shape.reduce_spc(big, 8);                                         % q = 64, 49,152 facets
sbtk.shape.write_obj(small, "g_00243mm_spc_obj_dimo_0000n00000_v004_every8.obj");

    Bodies#

    A body (body) is a shape model with its total mass and the reference radius of its gravity coefficients. sbtk.body.from_mass takes the mass; sbtk.body.create takes the bulk density and computes the mass from the volume. The bulk density is M/V; the background density of a model with components differs from it (Density distributions).

    MATLAB fragment. Requires the variables defined in Checking a shape model.
    body = sbtk.body.from_mass("Didymos", shape, 5.273e11, 0.43);    % mass [kg], reference radius [km]
sbtk.body.summary(body)
sbtk.plot.shape(body);
