    Bennu#

    Last edited October 19, 2026

    projects/bennu/ estimates the interior of the asteroid Bennu from the OSIRIS-REx gravity field (JPL, 2019; degree 10, Rref = 0.290 km) following Cavale et al., "Constraints on the Internal Density Distribution of Bennu Using OSIRIS-REx Mission Data" (Icarus), and reproduces that paper's inversion.

    Files#

    File Purpose
    bennu_body.m Shape model bennu_higher_qual.obj (196,608 facets, body-fixed frame with origin at the center of mass), bulk density 1190 kg/m³, Rref = 0.290 km.
    bennu_measurements.m The field as a measurement set, with the paper's weighting (C10 = C11 = S11 = 0, J2, C21, S21, C22, S22, J3, J4 with equal weights) or with all coefficients of degrees 2–4 and the full JPL covariance. Reads the file with sbtk.io.read_jpl_gravity.
    bennu_paper_components.m The paper's nominal model: prescribed surface (0–10 m, −297.5 kg/m³) and subsurface (10–20 m, +1190 kg/m³) layers, a core of radius 170 m with estimated center, an equatorial torus and four boulders. Positions are from the origin of the shape model, as in the paper; that origin is Bennu's center of mass from the OSIRIS-REx tracking data, 1.4 m from the center of figure (Where to place components).
    invert_bennu_paper.m Reproduces the paper's inversion with the background held at the bulk density and compares the result with the paper's values (tests/reference/bennu_paper_nominal.json).
    invert_bennu_boulder_positions.m The same with the centers of the two boulders that target C21/S21 and C22/S22 also estimated, with an a priori 1σ of 10 m per coordinate.
    check_bennu_full_field.m Compares the reproduced interior's coefficients of degrees 2–4 with the full field and covariance.
    bennu_compare_with_paper.m Prints a fit next to the paper's values.

    Reproduction of the paper#

    Relative densities from tools/make_readme_examples.m, which runs the paper configuration:

    Relative density (kg/m³) Paper SBTK
    core −241.9 −242.1
    torus −239.4 −234.6
    boulder C21 +664.5 +660.8
    boulder C22 −1049.3 −1037.3
    boulder north −148.0 −146.4
    boulder south −251.5 −250.9
    core center (m) (1.86, −7.89, 12.59) (2.06, −7.91, 12.56)

    The paper's code counted a surface facet as part of the torus only when all three of its vertices were inside the tube; SBTK integrates the exact intersection, whose volume is 2.4% larger, which accounts for most of the torus difference. tests/integration/test_bennu_paper.m checks the reproduction: the core relative density within 1 kg/m³, the boulders within 2%, the torus mass contrast within 1%, the core center within 0.5 m, and the coefficients the paper did not fit (C31 … S44) within 0.15 of the JPL 1σ (Validation).
