    ROB-DATA4: Dimorphos#

    Last edited October 27, 2026

    Result. C¯22 changes sign: the homogeneous body's elongation along x is cancelled by mass along ±y. Two families of interiors fit the coefficients and the inertia: smooth sector grids with denser material toward ±y (reduced χ2 1.7, p=0.05), and a dense core 31 m toward −y with a dense equatorial torus and a dense crust (0.65). The surface point at the −y end decides between them: the core model predicts the lander's acceleration to 0.2% and 0.2°, the sector grids miss it by 10%. The package's mass corresponds to 1540 kg/m³.

best: sectors 2 x 4 x 8
    • A core alone placed by its profile goes 52 m toward +y (reduced χ2 15), with a layer 38 m (15): the χ2 profile has its lowest point on the side opposite the center-of-mass offset, because one body cannot produce the change of C¯22.
    • A core with a torus and a dense layer: 0.65, with the core 31 m toward −y (+2530 ± 850 kg/m³), the torus +419 ± 170 and the crust +1790 ± 800 kg/m³. The torus and the crust put mass at the equator and outside, the core breaks the symmetry along y. The uncertainties are large: with 8 numbers resolved (degree 2 and three moments), the three densities and the position share them.
    • Two dense spheres along y (2.2) and an elongated dense core along y (6.0) come close but are rejected.
    • Sectors: one or two shells reach 1.7 with the largest consistent regularization, denser bands toward ±y and lighter toward ±x and the poles. The two-shell grid has the fewest effective parameters (4.8) and is listed as the best model by that rule.

    The point masses form a four-lobed pattern about the center: lighter along ±x, denser along ±y. The positive and negative parts both have their centroids at the center, so their mass-weighted centroids say nothing here: the anomaly is a quadrupole, the change of C¯22, not an offset mass.

    The surface point is at the −y end, 81 m from the center. The homogeneous body predicts 13% too little acceleration there. Of the four models consistent with the coefficients and the inertia, the two core models predict it to 0.2% and 0.7%, the two sector grids to −9.7% and −10.4%: the sectors spread the mass excess along ±y over the whole volume, the core model concentrates it toward −y, under the lander. The surface point selects the core model.
