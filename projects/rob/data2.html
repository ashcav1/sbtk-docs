    ROB-DATA2: Didymos#

    Last edited October 27, 2026

    Result. The density of this package changes with direction, not with depth. A grid of 32 sectors (one shell, 4 latitude and 8 longitude bands) fits every coefficient to degree 5 with the smallest variations the measurements accept (reduced χ2 1.65, p=0.05): a denser southern part, up to +230 ± 30 kg/m³ toward −x, and slightly lighter northern and +y parts. Fitted to degrees 1–4, it predicts degree 5. No combination of a core, a torus, a layer, an ellipsoid or two spheres comes close (reduced χ2 20 at best): the center of mass is 2.3 m from the center of figure and degree 3 carries most of the signal, which compact bodies near the center cannot produce. The package's mass corresponds to 2605 kg/m³.

best: sectors 1 x 4 x 8
    • A core placed by its χ2 profile goes 99 m from the center, toward −y (+680 kg/m³): reduced χ2 40. With a layer it moves in to 50 m and the layer becomes denser than the background by 2200 kg/m³ (a dense crust); with a torus whose height and axis are free, 20. Every one of these leaves degree 3 unexplained.
    • Compact bodies placed where the point masses put the anomaly (a dense sphere toward −x, −y, −z and a light one toward +y, +z): 27. Caps (a dense south and a light north): 900.
    • Sectors: 32 sectors of one shell reach a reduced χ2 of 1.65 with the largest regularization that is still consistent; with two shells, 1.65 as well, with the inner shell almost uniform. The measurements want lateral variations; they do not ask for radial ones.
