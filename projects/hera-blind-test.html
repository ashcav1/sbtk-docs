    Hera blind test#

    Last edited October 20, 2026

    projects/hera_hlp6/ contains the runner scripts SBTK uses in a blind test of interior-modeling methods for the binary asteroid Didymos–Dimorphos, the target of ESA's Hera mission. In such a test one team simulates measurements of an interior it keeps hidden and shares only the measurements; other teams estimate the interior; the hidden models are released afterwards and compared with the estimates on a common density grid. The makers deliver exact values, without uncertainties or noise; each receiving team weights them with uncertainties of its own choosing. SBTK's inversion runners use those of the Hera end-to-end simulation, read from files of the Hera working group that are not distributed with SBTK; the runners need them in data/hera_rse/.

    Files#

    File Purpose
    hlp6_didymos.m, hlp6_dimorphos.m Settings shared by every run of one body: shape model, mass, reference radius, degree, density-grid spacing.
    hlp6_team.m Team identifier and contact written into package names and files.
    hlp6_uncertainties.m The receiving team's uncertainties: the Hera radio-science precision of the gravity coefficients (to degree 5 for Didymos, 3 for Dimorphos, to which the coefficients are truncated), the inertia elements and the libration ratio.
    simulate_didymos.m, simulate_dimorphos.m Simulate measurements of a hidden interior; write the measurement package and the interior-model package.
    invert_didymos.m, invert_dimorphos.m Invert a measurement package (by default the redacted package of the newest simulation); write a solution package.
    invert_didymos_sectors.m The same with a 36-sector grid, the background held at the bulk density and regularization by the discrepancy principle.
    invert_received_data.m Invert another team's package (SBTK format) or a gravity file in the PDS SHADR layout.
    compare_didymos.m, compare_dimorphos.m Compare an inversion with the hidden model, by component and on the density grid.

    Simulation runs#

    A simulation runner has four sections:

    1. NAME AND DELIVERY: the run name, the package number n, and the inertia quantities the package contains (any of Ixx … Iyz, libration, or none; sbtk.data.select_moi).
    2. INTERIOR: components and random field (Density distributions), in the axes of the DART shape-model file with positions measured from the center of figure (cof = body.centroid_km), not from the origin of the file, which is 57 m from it for Didymos (Where to place components). The torus lies about the z-axis through cof. align_with_spin_axis names the components whose axis is set along the axis of the largest moment of the whole true model (Didymos: "torus", which forms about the spin axis; Dimorphos: none), with sbtk.interior.align_with_spin_axis.
    3. FRAME OF THE PACKAGES: the origin is the center of mass of the true model, as in mission body-fixed frames, and the axes are "spin", "principal" or "shape". Didymos uses the spin axes of the true model (z along its axis of the largest moment, x toward the shape-model x-axis: C¯21=S¯21=0, as in a mission field of a body in principal-axis rotation); Dimorphos, which may be tumbling, uses its principal axes (Frames of a gravity field).
    4. RUN: sbtk.interior.create and sbtk.interior.align_with_spin_axis; sbtk.data.simulate (the true model and its exact values, without uncertainties); with sbtk.gravity.center_of_mass_frame the center of mass and the axes of the chosen frame, and sbtk.body.to_frame, sbtk.interior.to_frame, sbtk.data.to_frame to carry the body (its shape model is moved and rotated and written into the package as <package>_<shape>_com_<frame>_axes.obj, a name no other package has, The shape model of a package), the true model and the exact values into it; sbtk.data.select_moi; then sbtk.io.report_simulation, sbtk.exchange.write_data (a package of exact values: no 1σ columns, no covariance file, Exchange packages) and sbtk.exchange.write_model.

    The run folder separates what may be shared from what may not:

    Simulation run folder of projects/hera_hlp6
    results/hera_hlp6/<date_time>_<name>/
  log.txt                          everything printed, including the true model
  redacted/<team>-DATA<n>-<body>/  the measurement package to share
  unredacted/<team>-MOD<n>-<body>/ the interior-model package, shared after the estimates
  unredacted/                      measurements.*, truth.*, settings.json, figures/

    Only the package folder in redacted/ is shared: log.txt and everything in unredacted/ contain the true model.

    Maker models#

    examples/make_didymos_1.m to make_dimorphos_3.m are six true models made the same way, one script each, into results/didymos_models/ and results/dimorphos_models/. They send exact values of every degree to 6 and Ixx, Iyy, Izz.

    Script Interior (relative densities)
    make_didymos_1 Underdense core, underdense equatorial torus aligned with the spin axis, underdense surface layer
    make_didymos_2 Model 1 and two dense boulders of 40 m, on the equator and at latitude 45°
    make_didymos_3 Model 1 and six dense boulders of 35 m, placed to excite C¯21, S¯21, C¯22, S¯22, J3 and C¯33
    make_dimorphos_1 Underdense surface layer and a denser sector on the side of the DART impact
    make_dimorphos_2 Model 1, a dense body below the impact site and two voids
    make_dimorphos_3 Model 2, an underdense fractured ellipsoid below the impact site and six voids

    The impact side is the side DART imaged on its approach, where the shape model has its finest detail; it is centered near latitude −11°, longitude 260° in the axes of the DART file. tests/integration/test_maker_scripts.m runs make_didymos_3 and make_dimorphos_3 and checks their packages.

    Inversion runs#

    invert_didymos.m reads the redacted package of the newest simulation of the given name with sbtk.exchange.locate and sbtk.exchange.read_data, exactly as another team receives it, so the inversion expands about the measured center of mass (Density estimation). hlp6_uncertainties.m then gives the exact values the receiving team's uncertainties (the Hera radio-science precision, truncating the coefficients to the degree it covers). Its model places the core and the torus at the center of figure of the package's shape model, as a receiving team would, and estimates the torus axis and height (Estimated geometry): estimate_axis = true, estimate_position = [false false true], with a priori 1σ of 1° and 10 m and limits of 10° and 50 m. It writes fit.*, homogeneous.txt, figures and the solution package <team>-SOL_<data team><n>/. invert_received_data.m does the same for a package from another team, or, with the commented lines, for a SHADR-layout gravity file, for which the shape model and the point the coefficients are about must be given.

    Comparison runs#

    compare_didymos.m loads the newest simulation and inversion of the given names and the body from the simulation's measurement package (so that all three are in the frame of the packages), compares the fit with the true model (sbtk.compare.with_truth) and compares the solution package's density grid with the interior-model package's (sbtk.compare.grids), which is the comparison every team's solution receives.

    Example runs#

    The delivered interiors (named T1) include a boulder and a random density field that the delivered inversion models do not contain. Weighted by the uncertainties of the end-to-end simulation, the χ2 test rejects the inversion models of both bodies, and the estimated densities are biased accordingly. At this precision an inversion model has to include structure down to the scale of such features to pass the test (Goodness of fit). For Didymos T1, whose torus is aligned with the spin axis and so lies about the z-axis of the package, the model with fixed geometry has a reduced χ2 of 121 and R2=−5.3. Estimating the torus axis and height lowers χ2 to 39 but R2 to −5.7: the torus tilts 5.6° and moves 41 m to take up the signal of the boulder, which the model lacks (Estimated geometry). The run takes about a minute. For Dimorphos, in its principal axes, the ellipsoidal core of the model is turned 12° about z against the true core, as a receiving team's would be (reduced χ2 313).
