    Didymos and Dimorphos values#

    Last edited October 21, 2026

    Every value SBTK uses to model Didymos and Dimorphos, where it comes from and where to find it. Each value is one of:

    • agreed: the value the Hera blind-test group uses;
    • server: from a file on the Hera working-group server (folder paths below are relative to its top folder);
    • published: from a paper or a public data product (references at the end of the page);
    • derived: computed from other values on this page;
    • assumed: an SBTK choice, not a measurement.

    The HLP6 makers agreed on the Didymos values on 21 October 2026 [HLP6 cross-validation 2026]: the 1.165 m shape model in its own frame, bulk density 2600 kg/m³, reference radius 410 m, degrees to 10, and the potential and acceleration at 20 given points. There are no agreed Dimorphos values yet.

    The values are set in the repository in projects/hera_hlp6/hlp6_didymos.m and projects/hera_hlp6/hlp6_dimorphos.m (shape model, mass or bulk density, reference radius, degree, grid spacing), projects/hera_hlp6/hlp6_uncertainties.m (the receiving team's uncertainties) and the maker scripts examples/make_didymos_1.m to make_dimorphos_3.m.

    Shape models#

    The shape models are the DART stereophotoclinometry (SPC) models, Didymos version 3 and Dimorphos version 4, from before the DART impact [Daly et al. 2023; Daly et al. 2024]. They are published in two places:

    • PDS: NASA Planetary Data System, Small Bodies Node, bundle pds4-dart_shapemodel-v1.0 [DART shape models 2023]. Each collection holds the global model at four samplings (OBJ files) with the digital terrain maps (FITS):
    • Didymos: https://pds-smallbodies.astro.umd.edu/holdings/pds4-dart_shapemodel-v1.0/data_derived_didymos_model_v003/
    • Dimorphos: https://pds-smallbodies.astro.umd.edu/holdings/pds4-dart_shapemodel-v1.0/data_derived_dimorphos_model_v004/
    • ESA SPICE: the full-resolution models only, as OBJ and digital shape kernel (.bds) files, https://spiftp.esac.esa.int/data/SPICE/HERA/kernels/dsk/. The working-group server has the same two files in Hera_WG/End-to-End Test/Shape/, and the agreed Didymos file in Hera_WG/End-to-End Test/HLP6G BlindTest/inputs/Reference models/Didymos/.

    The volume and the volume-equivalent diameter D=(6V/π)1/3 are derived from each file.

    Points to know:

    • Use the same file as the other teams. The agreed Didymos file is the 1.165 m model. The 9.3 m file is a separate product, not a subsample of it: its volume is 0.22% smaller, its center of figure is 0.35 m away, and its degree-2 coefficients differ by up to 3×10−4, about the size of the measurement uncertainties. Data made with one file and inverted with another carry that difference as an error. data/shapes/README.md compares all the versions.
    • Packages do not include the file. A package made with one of these files names it (Exchange packages).
    • Dimorphos. The models are from before the DART impact, which may have changed the shape.
    • Frame. Each file has its own body-fixed frame, z along the spin axis. The HLP6 makers keep it ("native reference frame of the shape model, not barycentric"; Frames of a gravity field). The center of figure is 56.765 m from the origin of the agreed Didymos file, at (+15.723, +42.487, −34.204) m, and 0.751 m from that of the Dimorphos file, at (−0.741, −0.117, −0.007) m.

    The maker packages give GM and the mass (GM/G). sbtk.exchange.read_data takes the mass from GM, so a package from a team that used another G gives that team's mass.

    Bulk density#

    The bulk density is the background density of a homogeneous model (Density distributions). For Didymos it is agreed, 2600 kg/m³, and sets the mass. For Dimorphos it is derived: 2.700 × 10⁹ kg over the volume of the 0.243 m model, 1534 kg/m³.

    Before the agreement SBTK took the mass of the RSE end-to-end test, 5.273 × 10¹¹ kg, which gives 2593 kg/m³ with the 9.3 m file and 2587 kg/m³ with the 1.165 m file.

    Potential and acceleration at the cross-validation points#

    The makers also give the gravitational potential and acceleration of the homogeneous Didymos at 20 points [HLP6 cross-validation 2026], in m in the frame of the agreed file (projects/hera_hlp6/cross_validation/points.csv):

    Points Distance from the origin of the file
    1: on the surface at latitude 45°, longitude 45° 332 m
    2: near an equilibrium point of the homogeneous body 360 m
    3–8, 9–14, 15–20: six points each on spheres of radius 1.2, 1.5 and 2.0 Rref (Fibonacci lattice) 492, 615, 820 m

    SBTK computes them exactly from the polyhedron of the shape model (Potential and acceleration at points). The sphere about the origin that encloses Didymos has a radius of 449.7 m, so points 1 and 2 are inside it, where the spherical-harmonic expansion does not converge. At 1.2 Rref the expansion to degree 10 differs from the exact potential by up to 1.5 × 10⁻⁴ of it; a team that evaluates the coefficients there will differ by that much. At point 2 the gravitational acceleration, 2.40 × 10⁻⁴ m/s², points to the center of mass and equals the centrifugal acceleration of the 2.26 h rotation about an axis through it: the point is an equilibrium point, as stated.

    Uncertainties of the receiving team#

    The makers send exact values. SBTK's inversion runners give them the precision of the RSE end-to-end test (projects/hera_hlp6/hlp6_uncertainties.m), read from the RSE files (server, Hera_WG/End-to-End Test/Radio Science/Gravity/, copies in data/hera_rse/, whose README.md describes them; the files are marked "test product only, not for scientific use"). The blind-test group's precision files are in Hera_WG/End-to-End Test/HLP6G BlindTest/inputs/uncertainties/ (UniBo-PREC1-Gravity_MOIs).

    The Didymos coefficients and their 1σ are given for Rref = 0.43 km; sbtk.data.use_uncertainties converts the 1σ of degree l to the agreed 0.41 km by the factor (0.43/0.41)l.

    The RSE fields are given in principal-axis frames (for the homogeneous body, the axis of the smallest moment is turned 32.2° (Didymos) and 7.8° (Dimorphos) from the shape-model x-axis toward −y, with the axis of the largest moment tilted 3.8° and 0.7° from the shape-model z-axis). Their 1σ table is applied in the axes of the packages without that rotation, which is an approximation (Frames of a gravity field).

    The RSE fields are also about the center of mass, while the packages are about the origin of the shape model file. The covariance is therefore moved to that point exactly, K′=TKT𝖳 (sbtk.data.use_uncertainties): moving a field to another point mixes the degrees, so the uncertainty of the center of mass (degree 1) enters the other coefficients. About the origin of the file, C¯20 of Didymos (UCB-DATA1) has 2.3 times the RSE 1σ and some degree-3 coefficients up to 1.8 times; C¯22 of Dimorphos (UCB-DATA4, center of mass 2.5 m from the origin) has 27 times the RSE 1σ, which is only 4×10−6. The inertia elements, about the same point, get the variance the center of mass adds through the parallel-axis theorem: negligible for Didymos, 12 to 19% more 1σ for Dimorphos. A team that applies the RSE 1σ unchanged to coefficients about the file origin weights those coefficients too much.
