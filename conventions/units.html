    Units#

    Last edited October 19, 2026

    SBTK uses one set of units in every function, structure and file. Every stored quantity that has a unit carries it at the end of its name; a name without a unit suffix is dimensionless.

    Quantity Unit Name suffix Example
    Lengths, positions, radii, depths km _km center_km = [0.02 0 0] (20 m along x)
    Densities and relative densities kg/m³ _kgm3 drho_kgm3 = -900
    Mass kg _kg mass_kg = 5.273e11
    Volume km³ _km3 volume_km3 = 0.20336
    Gravitational parameter GM km³/s² _km3_s2 GM_km3_s2 = 3.519e-8
    Angles: latitude, longitude, rotations degrees _deg lat_deg = [40 90]
    Gravity coefficients C¯lm, S¯lm dimensionless fully normalized (Gravity coefficients)
    Moments of inertia in measurement sets I/(MRref2), dimensionless _norm Izz≈0.38 for Didymos with Rref=0.43 km
    Moments of inertia in reports and text files kg m² _kgm2, _kg_m2 inertia_tensor_kg_m2
    Gravitational constant G km³ kg⁻¹ s⁻² 6.67430 × 10⁻²⁰ (CODATA 2018), sbtk.units.G

    sbtk.units.table prints this table in MATLAB.

    Where units are given at input#

    Two inputs carry a unit chosen by the caller:

    • Shape files. sbtk.shape.load and sbtk.shape.from_arrays require the length unit of the vertices, "km", "m" or "cm"; there is no default. sbtk.units.length_scale gives the conversion factor to km.
    • Moments of inertia in kg m². sbtk.units.moi_to_norm converts them to I/(MRref2) before sbtk.data.add_moi; sbtk.units.norm_to_moi converts back.

    All other arguments take the units of the table; their names say which (sbtk.interior.sphere(name, center_km, radius_km, drho_kgm3)).

    Conversions inside the toolkit#

    Volumes are in km³ and densities in kg/m³, so the mass of a region of density ρ and volume V is ρV×109 kg. Moments of inertia are normalized with Rref in km, so I/(MRref2) needs no further factor; converting to kg m² multiplies by M(103Rref)2.

    A center-of-mass offset d (km) along one axis corresponds to the fully normalized degree-1 coefficient d/(3Rref) (sbtk.units.offset_to_degree1); this is how a center-of-mass uncertainty enters sbtk.data.uncertainties.
