    Validation and tests#

    SBTK 1.0.0 has 129 unit and integration tests in MATLAB's unit-test framework. They run with

    MATLAB
    run(fullfile(sbtk.root, "tests", "run_tests.m"))      % about 3 minutes; writes tests/reports/test_report.txt

    This page states what the tests check and with what tolerance, as written in the test files. It makes no claims beyond them. Unit tests use a small ellipsoid body (tests/unit/small_body.m); validation tests use an ellipsoid with semi-axes 0.5 × 0.4 × 0.3 km (20,480 facets) and the 9.3 m Didymos model with a bulk density of 2600 kg/m³ and Rref = 0.40 km.

    Comparison with independent results#

    From tests/integration/test_validation.m:

    Test Check Tolerance
    testKernelVolume The kernel's degree-0 integral of every facet cone equals the cone volume. relative 10⁻¹²
    testEllipsoidClosedForm C¯20, C¯22 and the principal moments of a homogeneous triaxial ellipsoid against the closed forms C20=(2c2−a2−b2)/(10R2), C22=(a2−b2)/(20R2), I=M(b2+c2)/5, … relative 2 × 10⁻³ (20,480-facet mesh)
    testConeScaling Kernel output for a cone scaled by s=0.37 against sl+3 times the unscaled integrals. relative 10⁻¹³
    testSpherePointMassVsRays A sphere integrated as a point mass against the same sphere integrated along rays (as an ellipsoid). relative 10⁻³
    testTorus Volume and Izz of a buried torus against 2π2Ra2 and V(R2+3a2/4). relative 10⁻⁴ (volume), 10⁻³ (Izz)
    testShellSector A full-sphere sector between radial fractions 0.5 and 0.75 has volume (0.753−0.53)V. relative 10⁻¹²
    testSectorsTileBody The 24 sectors of a sector grid fill the body exactly once. relative 10⁻¹²
    testRandomFieldMonteCarlo Mass and degree-2 integrals of a random field (σ = 120 kg/m³, L = 100 m) on Didymos against Monte Carlo integration with 4 × 10⁶ points. 2% of the largest value
    testExactRecovery Exact Didymos data of a core (off-center), ridge and regolith: densities and the core center are recovered, starting with the core at the origin. 10⁻⁶ kg/m³, 10⁻⁷ km, χ2 < 10⁻¹⁰
    testExactRecoverySectors Exact data of a 24-sector interior with random densities are fitted by the same sectors. χ2 < 10⁻⁶, densities within 20 kg/m³
    testCalibration Over 40 noise realizations, the standard deviation of (estimate − truth)/σ. within 0.25 of 1
    testFullCovariance The same with coefficients correlated by 0.6 and noise drawn from that covariance. within 0.25 of 1
    testInertiaHelps Adding Ixx, Iyy, Izz to the gravity coefficients reduces every density uncertainty. strict
    testDimorphosExact Exact data of degree ≤ 3 and the libration ratio only (no other inertia data): an ellipsoidal core and a regolith layer are recovered. 10⁻⁶ kg/m³
    testTextRoundTrip Writing and reading the measurement text format preserves values, covariance, origin, seed and libration ratio, and the fits agree. relative 10⁻¹⁴; densities 10⁻⁶ kg/m³
    testFromCoefficients Data built from coefficient matrices equal the original measurement set and give the same fit. exact; 10⁻⁶ kg/m³

    Conventions and frames#

    From tests/unit/test_exchange.m and tests/unit/test_data.m:

    Test Check Tolerance
    testDegreeTwoAndInertia The relations between C¯20, C¯21, S¯21, C¯22, S¯22 and the inertia elements (Moments of inertia). 10⁻¹²
    testNormalizationCode The normalization function printed in conventions.txt gives N20,N21,N22,N30=5,5/3,5/12,7. relative 10⁻¹⁵
    testShTranslation Integrals moved by the solid-harmonic translation equal integrals computed about the shifted origin, including inertia. 10⁻¹² of the largest value
    testPredictionsAboutTheModelCenterOfMass For data about the center of mass, the predicted coefficients and inertia of a fit equal a forward model computed from scratch about the fitted center of mass. 10⁻⁶
    testCenterOfMassFrameMatchesTheTrueFrame Inverting a measurement package (expansion about the model's center of mass) gives the densities of an inversion about the true center of mass. 0.2σ; χ2 within 10%
    testFrameIterationWithSectors The center-of-mass iteration converges with layers and many sectors. offset < 10⁻⁶ km in fewer than 20 iterations
    testNothingAboutTheInterior A measurement package does not contain component names, the data-set name of the model, the seed, the expansion origin or the true center of mass.
    testGridDefinition Density-grid cell centers lie on multiples of the spacing and the cells fill the body; grids of different spacing are rejected. volume within 5% at 50 m cells
    testLibrationRatioUsesPrincipalMoments The simulated libration ratio equals (B−A)/C from the eigenvalues of the tensor, and the true tensor satisfies the libration row. relative 10⁻¹², 10⁻¹⁴
    testLibrationAmplitudeFormula G=2e/(3k−1) against numerical integration of planar libration in a Keplerian orbit (e = 0.01; k = 0.07, 0.25, 0.5), and the inverse formula. 3%; relative 10⁻¹²

    Inversion behavior#

    From tests/unit/test_invert.m: exact recovery with ν=n−p; the mass constraint (relative 10⁻¹⁰); the homogeneous fit has the bulk density and ν=n; regularization reduces the norm of the relative densities and increases the degrees of freedom; the p-value of χ2=10 with ν=10 equals 0.44049; density bounds hold and are flagged; settings by name and value; starting values of the relative densities are not used; unknown inertia labels and inconsistent replacement data are rejected.

    Reproduction of published and earlier results#

    Test file Check Tolerance
    tests/integration/test_bennu_paper.m The Bennu inversion of Cavale et al. (Icarus) (Bennu): core relative density; boulders; torus mass contrast; core center; background equal to the bulk density 1190 kg/m³; coefficients of degrees 3–4 not fitted by the paper. 1 kg/m³; 2%; 1%; 0.5 m; 10⁻⁹ kg/m³; 0.15 of the JPL 1σ
    tests/integration/test_regression_baseline.m Geometry, integrals, sample points, simulation, noise, inversion, regularization choice, comparison and position estimation against values stored from the code before the toolkit (tests/reference/baseline_v0.mat). relative 10⁻¹⁰ unless stated in the test

    Other tests#

    Test file Contents
    tests/unit/test_units.m G, length scales, inertia conversions, degree-1 conversion.
    tests/unit/test_shape.m OBJ reading with units, write–read round trip, ellipsoid volume (relative 3 × 10⁻³) and centroid, detection of inward and open meshes, units required, bundled models, SPC reduction (closed, outward, star-shaped).
    tests/unit/test_body.m Mass from density, centroid of a shifted shape, bodies from mass, invalid densities rejected.
    tests/unit/test_interior.m Constructors, ellipsoid rotation, layer depths, latitude–longitude, duplicate names, sector grids and ranges, random field reproducible and with standard deviation within 15% of σ.
    tests/unit/test_gravity.m Uniform body, mass kept by the background, center-of-mass shift, labels, non-star-shaped meshes rejected, components clipped to the body.
    tests/unit/test_data.m Measurement fields, center-of-mass frame, noise, negative density rejected, selection, covariance, inertia and libration rows, inertia uncertainties.
    tests/unit/test_compare.m R2=1 for the true model; R2=0 (within 0.01) for a homogeneous estimate.
    tests/unit/test_io.m Run folders, missing runs, report functions.
    tests/unit/test_plot.m Every figure function runs.
    tests/integration/test_examples.m The eight tutorial scripts run, ex01 also section by section.
    tests/integration/test_template_project.m The project template runs: simulation, inversion, comparison.

    Longer studies in tests/studies/ (recovery over interiors and data sets, edge cases of the forward model against brute-force integration, uncertainty calibration) are run separately and are not part of the test suite.
