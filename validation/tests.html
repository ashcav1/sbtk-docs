    Validation and tests#

    Last edited October 28, 2026

    SBTK 1.0.0 has 168 unit and integration tests in MATLAB's unit-test framework. They run with

    MATLAB
    run(fullfile(sbtk.root, "tests", "run_tests.m"))      % about 30 minutes; writes tests/reports/test_report.txt

    Most of the time goes to tests/integration/test_maker_scripts.m, which runs three HLP6 maker scripts on the full-resolution shape models (3 million facets, degree 10).

    This page states what the tests check and with what tolerance, as written in the test files. It makes no claims beyond them. Unit tests use a small ellipsoid body (tests/unit/small_body.m); validation tests use an ellipsoid with semi-axes 0.5 × 0.4 × 0.3 km (20,480 facets) and the 9.3 m Didymos model with a bulk density of 2600 kg/m³ and Rref = 0.40 km.

    Comparison with independent results#

    From tests/integration/test_validation.m:

    Test Check Tolerance
    testKernelVolume The kernel's degree-0 integral of every facet cone equals the cone volume. relative 10⁻¹²
    testEllipsoidClosedForm C¯20, C¯22 and the principal moments of a homogeneous triaxial ellipsoid against the closed forms C20=(2c2−a2−b2)/(10R2), C22=(a2−b2)/(20R2), I=M(b2+c2)/5, … relative 2 × 10⁻³ (20,480-facet mesh)
    testConeScaling Kernel output for a cone scaled by s=0.37 against sl+3 times the unscaled integrals. relative 10⁻¹³
    testSpherePointMassVsRays A sphere integrated as a point mass against the same sphere integrated along rays (as an ellipsoid). relative 10⁻³
    testTorus Volume and Izz of a buried torus against 2π2Ra2 and V(R2+3a2/4). relative 10⁻⁴ (volume), 10⁻³ (Izz)
    testShellSector A full-sphere sector between radial fractions 0.5 and 0.75 has volume (0.753−0.53)V. relative 10⁻¹²
    testSectorsTileBody The 24 sectors of a sector grid fill the body exactly once. relative 10⁻¹²
    testRandomFieldMonteCarlo Mass and degree-2 integrals of a random field (σ = 120 kg/m³, L = 100 m) on Didymos against Monte Carlo integration with 4 × 10⁶ points. 2% of the largest value
    testExactRecovery Exact Didymos data of a core (off-center), torus and regolith: densities and the core center are recovered, starting with the core at the origin. 10⁻⁶ kg/m³, 10⁻⁷ km, χ2 < 10⁻¹⁰
    testExactRecoverySectors Exact data of a 24-sector interior with random densities are fitted by the same sectors. χ2 < 10⁻⁶, densities within 20 kg/m³
    testCalibration Over 40 noise realizations, the standard deviation of (estimate − truth)/σ. within 0.25 of 1
    testFullCovariance The same with coefficients correlated by 0.6 and noise drawn from that covariance. within 0.25 of 1
    testInertiaHelps Adding Ixx, Iyy, Izz to the gravity coefficients reduces every density uncertainty. strict
    testDimorphosExact Exact data of degree ≤ 3 and the libration ratio only (no other inertia data): an ellipsoidal core and a regolith layer are recovered. 10⁻⁶ kg/m³
    testTextRoundTrip Writing and reading the measurement text format preserves values, covariance, origin, seed and libration ratio, and the fits agree. relative 10⁻¹⁴; densities 10⁻⁶ kg/m³
    testFromCoefficients Data built from coefficient matrices equal the original measurement set and give the same fit. exact; 10⁻⁶ kg/m³

    Conventions and frames#

    From tests/unit/test_exchange.m and tests/unit/test_data.m:

    Test Check Tolerance
    testDegreeTwoAndInertia The relations between C¯20, C¯21, S¯21, C¯22, S¯22 and the inertia elements (Moments of inertia). 10⁻¹²
    testNormalizationCode The normalization function printed in conventions.txt gives N20,N21,N22,N30=5,5/3,5/12,7. relative 10⁻¹⁵
    testShTranslation Integrals moved by the solid-harmonic translation equal integrals computed about the shifted origin, including inertia; the predictions move degree ≥ 2 for data about the center of mass and every degree for data about a fixed point. 10⁻¹² of the largest value
    testUseUncertaintiesAboutAnotherPoint A reference covariance about another point is moved to the data's point (TKT𝖳, the same as sbtk.data.expand_about), with the correlations it brings; a reference without degree 1 is rejected. 10⁻¹² of the largest value
    testPredictionsAboutTheModelCenterOfMass For data about the center of mass, the predicted coefficients and inertia of a fit equal a forward model computed from scratch about the fitted center of mass. 10⁻⁶
    testCenterOfMassFrameMatchesTheTrueFrame Inverting a measurement package (expansion about the model's center of mass) gives the densities of an inversion about the true center of mass. 0.2σ; χ2 within 10%
    testFrameIterationWithSectors The center-of-mass iteration converges with layers and many sectors. offset < 10⁻⁶ km in fewer than 20 iterations
    testNothingAboutTheInterior A measurement package does not contain component names, the data-set name of the model, the seed, the expansion origin or the true center of mass.
    testExactPackage A package of exact values has the columns l,m,C,S, no covariance file and no uncertainty or noise lines; it is read back with NaN uncertainties, the inversion asks for uncertainties, and with them it fits. values 10⁻¹⁷; χ2 < 10⁻⁶
    testPackageAboutTheShapeOrigin A set expanded about the origin of the shape model file is written with degree 1 and the moments of inertia about that origin, read back as it is (coef_about = 'origin'), and inverted without a change of frame. values 10⁻¹⁷; χ2 < 10⁻⁶
    testReportStatesWhatApplies Uncertainties, noise and the libration ratio are stated only when the package has them; exact values with the center of mass as origin in principal axes, or with x, y turned 20° about z, are described as such in conventions.txt, and the package's shape model has its center of mass at the origin. 10⁻¹² km; obj precision for the center of figure
    testUnnormalizedCoefficients gravity_field_unnormalized.txt gives the coefficients of gravity_field.txt times Nlm, and their 1σ for values with uncertainties. relative 10⁻¹⁴
    testShippedShapeModelNotIncluded A package made with one of SBTK's shape model files names it but does not include it; sbtk.exchange.read_data reads it from data/shapes/. vertices exact
    testWritePotential potential_acceleration.txt gives the positions, potential and acceleration of sbtk.gravity.polyhedron in SI units and is listed once in README.txt. relative 10⁻¹⁴
    testGridDefinition Density-grid cell centers lie on multiples of the spacing and the cells fill the body; grids of different spacing are rejected. volume within 5% at 50 m cells
    testLibrationRatioUsesPrincipalMoments The simulated libration ratio equals (B−A)/C from the eigenvalues of the tensor, and the true tensor satisfies the libration row. relative 10⁻¹², 10⁻¹⁴
    testLibrationAmplitudeFormula G=2e/(3k−1) against numerical integration of planar libration in a Keplerian orbit (e = 0.01; k = 0.07, 0.25, 0.5), and the inverse formula. 3%; relative 10⁻¹²

    Inversion behavior#

    From tests/unit/test_invert.m: exact recovery with ν=n−p; the mass constraint (relative 10⁻¹⁰); the homogeneous fit has the bulk density and ν=n; regularization reduces the norm of the relative densities and increases the degrees of freedom; the p-value of χ2=10 with ν=10 equals 0.44049; density bounds hold and are flagged; settings by name and value; starting values of the relative densities are not used; unknown inertia labels and inconsistent replacement data are rejected. testEstimateTorusAxisAndHeight: the axis of a torus (true tilt 4.5°) and its height (12 m), started 3.5° and 15 m away, are recovered from exact values to 0.02° and 0.2 m with its density (5 kg/m³). testGeometryLimits: a hard limit on the tilt holds, an a priori constraint keeps the axis at its start, and geometry asked of components without a center or an axis is rejected. testEstimateEllipsoidOrientation: the full orientation of an ellipsoid (the direction of its third axis and its turn about it), started 8° away, is recovered from exact values to 0.05°, and a limit of 3° on the turn holds. The sphere-position results of test_regression_baseline.m (including the number of iterations) did not change when the estimated geometry was generalized.

    Reproduction of published and earlier results#

    Test file Check Tolerance
    tests/integration/test_bennu_paper.m The Bennu inversion of Cavale et al. (Icarus) (Bennu): core relative density; boulders; torus mass contrast; core center; background equal to the bulk density 1190 kg/m³; coefficients of degrees 3–4 not fitted by the paper. 1 kg/m³; 2%; 1%; 0.5 m; 10⁻⁹ kg/m³; 0.15 of the JPL 1σ
    tests/integration/test_regression_baseline.m Geometry, integrals, sample points, simulation, noise, inversion, regularization choice, comparison and position estimation against values stored from the code before the toolkit (tests/reference/baseline_v0.mat). Results that include an ellipsoid or a torus differ because their ray crossings are now exact: the old code sampled 256 points per chord and missed rays that only graze them (the exact crossings equal 4096 samples to 10⁻¹⁵). relative 10⁻¹⁰ unless stated in the test; 2 × 10⁻⁶ with an ellipsoid or a torus

    Other tests#

    Test file Contents
    tests/unit/test_units.m G, length scales, inertia conversions, degree-1 conversion.
    tests/unit/test_shape.m OBJ reading with units, write–read round trip, ellipsoid volume (relative 3 × 10⁻³) and centroid, detection of inward and open meshes, units required, bundled models, SPC reduction (closed, outward, star-shaped).
    tests/unit/test_body.m Mass from density, centroid of a shifted shape, bodies from mass, invalid densities rejected.
    tests/unit/test_interior.m Constructors, ellipsoid rotation, layer depths, latitude–longitude, duplicate names, sector grids and ranges, random field reproducible and with standard deviation within 15% of σ. testSetAxis: the axis points along the direction, by the smallest rotation. testAlignWithSpinAxis: a torus aligned with the axis of the largest moment of the model it belongs to (to 0.002°, in fewer than 10 simulations), the other components unchanged, unknown names and non-convergence reported.
    tests/unit/test_invert.m (additions) testLayersFromMeasuredCenterOfMass: data about a point 50 m from the center of mass, inverted with exactly the components of the true model (a core, a layer and a sector), give its densities (to 10⁻⁴ kg/m³), the measured center of mass as origin and residuals below 10⁻³σ. testMassAfterCenterOfMassIterations: data about the center of mass and an estimated component the body clips: after the center-of-mass iterations the fitted model has the measured mass (to 10⁻¹²).
    tests/unit/test_gravity.m Uniform body, mass kept by the background, center-of-mass shift, labels, non-star-shaped meshes rejected, components clipped to the body. testFieldAt also: a sphere inside the body gives the field of a point mass outside it and of a uniform ball inside it, exactly (to 10⁻¹²); one through the surface, on the grid, within 2%. testOneRayGrazingSphere: a sphere on the surface whose bounding sphere only one ray crosses, and that ray misses it, has volume 0. testPolyhedronAgreesWithCube: the polyhedron formula and the cube formula give the same potential and acceleration of a cube, outside, on a face (on the edge between its triangles) and inside (to 10⁻¹⁰). testPolyhedronField: inside the body the divergence of the acceleration is −4πGρ, outside the acceleration is the gradient of the potential (to 10⁻⁶), and far away the potential is GM/r. testFieldAt: a homogeneous model gives the polyhedron (to 10⁻¹²); a sphere of excess density adds the field of its mass (within 2% on a 10 m grid). testIndexedSurfaceDistance: facets sorted by direction give the surface distance of the search over every facet (to 10⁻¹³ km), about the center of figure and a point off it, also along the edges of the cube map. testSmallOverhangAccepted: seen from a point where one facet of a 0.1 mm dent faces it, the signed cones give the volume (to 10⁻¹²) and the center of figure (to 10⁻¹² km); a point outside the body is rejected. testCenterOfMassFrame: about the center of mass degree 1 is 0; in principal axes C¯21=S¯21=S¯22=0, each degree's power and each degree's total variance are unchanged, and C¯20, C¯22 equal the values from the principal moments; a measurement set, the same set without degree 1, and a set lacking a coefficient.
    tests/unit/test_interpret.m testHomogeneousBodyHasNoAnomaly: a homogeneous body about any point has no anomaly (to 10⁻¹²), degree scaling 1 and no center-of-mass offset. testAnomalyIndependentOfExpansionPoint: measurements about the center of mass, about another point and the true model give the same anomaly (to 10⁻¹²). testAnomalyCovariance: the covariance moves with the measurements; the center-of-mass 1σ is 3R times that of degree 1. testCentralMassOfOneSphere: degrees 1 and 2 of one sphere give its mass excess (to 10⁻⁹) and center (to 10⁻⁹ km). testCentralMassUncertainty: the 1σ of the mass excess matches the spread over 60 noise realizations (within 30%). testSignaturesAddUp: the anomaly of a model is the sum of its densities times their signatures. testSelectFindsTheSphere: forward selection picks the true sphere from four candidates with its density. testPredictReproducesTheFit: a fit to degree 1 and J2 predicts its own rows as the fit does and the other coefficients of the true model to χ2<10−6. testPredictNeedsTheSameCenterOfMass: a fit to data about another point is refused. testRecenterMatchesDegreeOne: the fixed-point iteration moves a sphere to its true center (to 1 mm). testScanPositionFindsTheCenter: the χ2 profile is 0 at the true center. testPointMassesFindTheAnomaly: point masses fitted to a dense sphere keep the mass and put their positive part within 50 m of it; with noise, the consistent rule leaves at least one degree of freedom, and the AIC rule's λ has a lower χ² + 2 p_eff than half or twice that λ. testReducedShapeKeepsTheInterior: the anomaly on a coarser model of the surface equals the original's (within 5%). testGeometryOfOneSphere: for a dense sphere in a round body the offset points at it (to 10⁻⁶ deg), degree 2 is an axis through it (to 1°) and the excess of degrees 2–4 points at it (within 3°). testGeometryOfATorus: the smallest axis of degree 2 of a torus about z is z (within 5°), with a 1σ below 1°. testPointMassesFindTheAnomaly also: the point-mass solution as a model gives its own χ2; with the consistent rule and no consistent smoothing, the best p-value is reported and the fit marked not consistent.
    tests/unit/test_search.m testParameterChecks: unknown components, fields and value widths are refused. testDirections: the axis first, unit vectors, the tilts as given. testGridMatchesRun: exact data pick the true layer thickness; with noise, the densities without bounds give the χ2 of sbtk.invert.run (to 10⁻⁶). testGridFindsTorus: the true ring and tube radii and the tilted axis among 84 combinations, χ2<10−8. testGridStartsInEachValley: the profile of a core with a layer has a valley on each side (a dense and a light core), and both are starts. testRefineFromSeveralStarts: Gauss–Newton from two starts recovers the core. testAddMassesFindsBoulder: one mass is added, at the hidden boulder (to 0.1 m) with its density, and the search stops when the model is consistent. testRunAllSteps: grid, Gauss–Newton and masses recover a core, a layer and a boulder from exact data; the figures draw.
    tests/unit/test_frames.m testSpinAxes: in the spin axes C¯21=S¯21=0 to 10−13, z is the axis of the largest moment, x lies in the plane of the shape-model x-axis and z, 12atan2(S¯22,C¯22) is the longitude of the axis of the smallest moment, each degree's power is unchanged, and the comparison with the homogeneous body gives its own terms. testChangeOfFrameEqualsSimulationInIt: a body, an interior with every component type and a random field, carried into a rotated and shifted frame and simulated there, give the transformed true model, coefficients, inertia rows and libration ratio of the original simulation (to 10−7), and the density grid in the new frame equals the original model at the same points (to 10−9 kg/m³). testPackageInSpinAxes: packages in the spin axes contain their own shape-model file, the data have C¯21, S¯21 zero within the noise, and the grid and the component descriptions are in the same frame. testChangeOfFrameRules: a shift applies to any set; new axes need exact inertia rows and complete degrees; fits are not transformed; non-rotations are rejected. testNoLocalPathInPackage: a package with a shape model changed in frame names the original file but not its folder. testChangesOfFrameCombine: after two changes of frame the shape model's vertices equal (original vertices - frame_origin_km)*frame_axes to 10⁻¹² km. testGeneratedShapeNamedAfterPackage: a shape model changed in frame and written into two packages gets two file names, <data set>_<shape name>.obj, and the interior-model package names the same file as its data package.
    tests/unit/test_data.m testExpandAbout: coefficients and inertia expanded about another point equal those computed about that point for a homogeneous body (to 10⁻¹⁴); incomplete degrees and non-element inertia rows are rejected. Measurement fields, center-of-mass frame, simulation without uncertainties and sbtk.data.set_uncertainties, noise, negative density rejected, selection, covariance, inertia and libration rows, inertia uncertainties.
    tests/unit/test_compare.m R2=1 for the true model; R2=0 (within 0.01) for a homogeneous estimate.
    tests/unit/test_io.m Run folders, missing runs, report functions; a run that only makes a true model (simulation without uncertainties, the comparison with the homogeneous body in principal axes, the interior-model package with the principal axes and moments of the true model).
    tests/unit/test_plot.m Every figure function runs (testInterpretationPlots: the anomaly, degree-scaling, significance, residual-map, point-mass, coefficient-comparison (with and without solutions), contribution figures and the solutions side by side). testDifferenceFromHomogeneous: a homogeneous body differs from itself by 0; one interior as a true model, a package, a field about the shape-model origin and a fitted model gives the same differences. testDifferenceInPrincipalAxes: the rotated field has C¯21=S¯21=S¯22=0 to 10−13, and the homogeneous field of the rotated shape model equals the rotated homogeneous coefficients to 10−12.
    tests/integration/test_examples.m The eleven tutorial scripts run, ex01 also section by section.
    tests/integration/test_template_project.m The project template runs: simulation, inversion, comparison.
    tests/integration/test_maker_scripts.m The seven HLP6 maker scripts in examples/ pass the code analyzer; make_didymos_3, make_dimorphos_3 and make_didymos_homogeneous run on the full-resolution models, and their packages have exact values only, to degree 10, normalized and unnormalized, in the frame and about the origin of the shape model file (degree 1 listed, C¯21, S¯21 not zero), which they name and do not include, every boulder or void of the model, and no local paths. The homogeneous package has the agreed GM and reference radius, its degree 1 gives the center of figure (to 10⁻⁹ km), it has all six inertia elements and the potential and acceleration at the 20 points of the makers' cross-validation.

    Longer studies in tests/studies/ (recovery over interiors and data sets, edge cases of the forward model against brute-force integration, uncertainty calibration) are run separately and are not part of the test suite.
